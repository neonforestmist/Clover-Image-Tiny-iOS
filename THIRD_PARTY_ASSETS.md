# Third-party visual assets

The following SVG assets were downloaded through the
[Iconify API](https://iconify.design/docs/api/) from the
[Phosphor Icons](https://phosphoricons.com/) collection:

- `ph:clover-fill` — Clover
- `ph:paint-brush-broad-fill` — Monet
- `ph:drop-half-bottom-fill` — Watercolor Anime
- `ph:dots-nine-bold` — Pointillism

the iOS asset catalog.
